## 1. Company Overview  
Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles Ltd (TMPV) is the passenger‑car arm of the Tata Motors Group. It designs, manufactures and sells a full range of passenger cars, SUVs and electric vehicles (EVs) for the Indian market and exports to a handful of overseas markets (UK, Europe, US, China, etc.).  

* **Revenue model** – Primarily **B2C** sales of complete vehicles; ancillary B2C revenue from spare‑parts, accessories and after‑sales services. A modest **B2B** component exists through the sale of engines & factory‑automation equipment and through its financing arm (Tata Capital).  
* **Geographic exposure** – ~85 % of sales are domestic (India); the remainder is export‑driven, mainly premium Jaguar Land Rover (JLR) models.  
* **Competitive landscape** – Competes with Maruti Suzuki, Mahindra & Mahindra, Hyundai, Kia and the premium segment (Toyota, Honda, BMW, etc.). The JLR business gives TMPV a **luxury‑segment moat** (brand, technology, global platform).

## 9. Risks & Red Flags  

1. **Cyclical demand** – Passenger‑car sales are highly sensitive to GDP growth, credit conditions and fuel prices. A macro slowdown could compress volumes.  
2. **One‑off “other income”** – FY‑25/26 results contain large non‑recurring items (₹88,669 Cr other income). Adjusted earnings are lower; analysts must strip these out.  
3. **EV execution risk** – Scaling EV production, battery supply and charging infrastructure remain uncertain; capital intensity could rise again.  
4. **Margin pressure from raw‑material cost** – Steel, aluminium and semiconductor shortages could erode operating margins if not passed on.
