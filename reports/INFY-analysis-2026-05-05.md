## 1. Company Overview
Infosys Ltd. is a **global IT services & consulting firm** headquartered in Bengaluru, India. It delivers end‑to‑end digital transformation services – cloud, AI (including generative AI), analytics, cybersecurity, ERP (SAP, Oracle, Salesforce), engineering, and industry‑specific platforms (e.g., Finacle for banking).  

* **Revenue model:** Primarily **B2B** – long‑term contracts, time‑and‑materials, and outcome‑based engagements with large enterprises across banking, manufacturing, retail, energy, telecom, and public‑sector clients.  
* **Geographic exposure:** ~55 % of revenue from **North America**, ~20 % Europe, the remainder from Asia‑Pacific and emerging markets.  
* **Market positioning:** 2nd largest Indian IT services firm (behind TCS), with a strong brand, deep domain expertise, and a growing AI‑first services suite (Topaz, Aster).

## 9. Risks & Red Flags  

1. **Revenue growth slowdown** – FY‑24 growth fell to 4 % YoY; any prolonged low‑single‑digit growth could pressure margins.  
2. **High valuation of P/B** – market may be pricing in future AI‑driven earnings acceleration; a miss on AI initiatives could cause a multiple contraction.  
3. **Promoter holding below 15 %** – relatively low, could be perceived as weaker alignment, though institutional ownership is strong.  
4. **Currency risk** – ~55 % of earnings are USD‑denominated; a sustained INR appreciation could compress margins.  
5. **Talent attrition** – IT services are labor‑intensive; rising attrition could increase hiring costs and affect delivery quality.

---

## 10. Final Verdict  

**Bullish (Long‑Term)**  

*Rationale:* Infosys delivers **high, stable profitability**, **excellent capital returns**, and **minimal leverage**. It trades **15‑16× P/E**, a discount to peers, while offering a **4 % dividend** and a clear AI‑first growth narrative. The modest slowdown in revenue growth is offset by margin resilience and strong cash generation. The upside potential lies in scaling digital platforms and continued AI adoption.
