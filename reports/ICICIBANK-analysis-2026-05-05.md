**Conclusion:** Leverage is typical for the sector; capital adequacy is strong. Contingent liabilities merit monitoring.

## 9. Risks & Red Flags  

1. **Credit‑quality risk** – Gross NPA fell to 1.40 % (FY‑26) but still above the “very low” threshold; any macro slowdown could push NPAs higher.  
2. **Contingent liabilities** – ₹6.2 T of contingent exposures; large off‑balance‑sheet risk that could materialise in stressed scenarios.  
3. **Interest‑rate environment** – Rising global rates may compress NIM if deposit costs rise faster than loan yields.  
4. **Regulatory risk** – RBI may tighten capital or provisioning requirements; banks are highly regulated.  
5. **Share‑float increase** – Public float jump to 25 % could increase price volatility and dilute existing shareholders if new shares are issued.
