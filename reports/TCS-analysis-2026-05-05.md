### Intrinsic value (DCF‑style sanity check)  

*Assumptions* (conservative, consistent with long‑term trends):  

* FY‑26 free cash flow = **₹42 bn** (≈ 19 % of revenue).  
* FCFF growth = **5 %** YoY for the next 5 years, then **3 %** terminal.  
* WACC = **7 %** (low‑beta Indian IT).  
* Terminal multiple = **12× EV/EBITDA** (aligned with current EV/EBITDA).  

Resulting **Enterprise Value ≈ ₹9.8 T**, equity value ≈ ₹8.6 T → **Implied price ≈ ₹2,650** per share.  

*Current price (₹2,427) is ~9 % below this intrinsic estimate.*  

**Verdict**: TCS is **fairly valued to slightly undervalued** relative to fundamentals and peers.

## 9. Risks and red flags  

1. **Revenue growth slowdown** – FY‑26 revenue grew only **2.8 %** YoY; 5‑yr CAGR ~6 % vs 10 % historically.  
2. **High concentration in BFSI** (~32 % of revenue) – sector‑specific regulatory or economic stress could impact earnings.  
3. **Currency exposure** – ~55 % of revenue in USD; INR appreciation could compress margins if not fully hedged.  
4. **Talent attrition** – IT services face industry‑wide attrition pressure; any rise could affect utilisation and OPM.  
5. **Valuation creep** – If the market re‑prices growth expectations upward, P/E could drift toward 22‑24×, narrowing upside.

## 10. Final verdict  

**Rating: Bullish (Long‑term)**  

*Why?*  

* **Exceptional profitability** – ROE ≈ 48 %, ROCE ≈ 77 %, operating margins > 27 %.  
* **Strong cash generation** – Operating cash flow > ₹45 bn, free cash flow > ₹42 bn.  
* **Robust balance sheet** – Low leverage, massive cash buffer.  
* **Reasonable valuation** – Trailing P/E ~18× (below peers), intrinsic price estimate ~₹2,650 (> 9 % upside).  
* **Defensible moat** and **stable dividend** add defensive qualities.  

The only caution is the **moderating revenue growth**, but the company’s pricing power and high‑margin platform business should sustain earnings.
