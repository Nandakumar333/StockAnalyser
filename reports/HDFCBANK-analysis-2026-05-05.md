*Notes*  

* **Borrowings** are largely **customer deposits** (the bank’s primary funding source). The “Borrowings” line in the balance sheet reflects RBI‑mandated statutory liquidity and other borrowings, not long‑term debt.

* **Interest coverage** is low (interest expense ≈ ₹10‑12 bn vs profit before tax ≈ ₹70‑95 bn → coverage ≈ 6‑8×). Screener flags “low interest coverage” but the ratio is still healthy for a bank.  

**Conclusion:** Leverage is **declining**; capital base is strong (CET1 > 15 % per RBI norms). No red‑flag debt build‑up.

## 9. Risks & Red Flags  

1. **Asset‑Quality Pressure** – Net NPA rose from 0.27 % (Mar‑23) to 0.38 % (Mar‑26). Still low, but any acceleration could hit margins.  
2. **Regulatory Rate‑Risk** – RBI may tighten policy rates; higher deposit rates could compress net interest margin if loan growth slows.  
3. **Contingent Liabilities** – Large off‑balance‑sheet exposures (e.g., government‑linked guarantees) could materialise in stressed scenarios.  
4. **Capital Adequacy** – Although CET1 is comfortably above 15 %, any unexpected loss could erode the buffer; monitor capital ratios quarterly.

## 10. Final Verdict  

**Rating: Bullish (Long‑Term)**  

*Why*  

* Valuation is **discounted** vs peers (P/E ≈ 17 vs ≈ 20) and below intrinsic DCF estimate.  
* **Profitability** is high and stable (net margins > 22 % in recent years).  
* **Return metrics** (ROE ≈ 15 %) exceed the 15 % quality threshold.  
* **Leverage** is improving; equity base expanded dramatically in FY‑24, reducing debt‑to‑equity to ~0.3.  
* **Moat** (scale, brand, distribution) is durable.  
* **Dividend** is growing, providing cash‑return component.  

The upside potential (target ~₹ 1,040) versus current price (~₹ 772) is ~ 35 %, while downside risk is limited by strong balance sheet and earnings resilience.
