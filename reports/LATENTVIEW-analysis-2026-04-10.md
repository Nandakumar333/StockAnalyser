## 1. Company overview  
Latent View Analytics Ltd. is a **business‑analytics & data‑engineering services firm**. It delivers consulting, advanced predictive/AI‑driven analytics, data‑platform modernisation, and visualisation solutions to **blue‑chip clients** across Technology, BFSI, CPG & Retail, Industrials and other sectors.  

* **Business model** – Project‑based and recurring‑service contracts (often multi‑year) paid in cash. Revenue is earned from professional services (high‑margin) and from longer‑term managed‑services/technology‑platform licences (lower but recurring).  
* **Customer mix** – Highly concentrated on large corporates; no direct consumer (B2C) exposure.  
* **Geography** – India (core), United States, United Kingdom, Singapore, Netherlands – giving modest export exposure but the bulk of revenue is domestic.  
* **Competitive landscape** – Competes with global consulting firms (Accenture, Deloitte, Capgemini) and niche analytics boutiques. Moat is **client‑relationship depth, proprietary data‑science IP and a growing AI‑practice**.

## 9. Risks and red flags  

1. **Margin compression** – Op‑margin fell from 50 % (FY‑21) to 20 % (TTM). If cost discipline does not improve, profitability could stay muted.  
2. **Revenue concentration** – Top‑10 clients likely > 30 % of revenue (typical for boutique consultancies). Loss of a major client would materially hit earnings.  
3. **Valuation premium** – P/E ~31× and P/B ~4.9× are above sector averages; any slowdown in growth could trigger a price correction.  
4. **Low ROE/ROCE** – Returns are well under the 15 % benchmark, indicating capital is not being deployed at high efficiency.  
5. **Quarterly volatility** – FY‑2025 saw a negative operating profit in Q1‑FY‑2025; while recovered, it shows earnings can be lumpy.

## 10. Final verdict  

**Neutral (Hold) for long‑term investing**  

*Rationale:* The company has **strong top‑line growth**, a **clean balance sheet**, and **reasonable cash conversion**. However, **profitability has weakened**, returns are modest, and the stock trades at a **valuation premium**. The upside is limited (~10‑15 % intrinsic upside) while downside risk from margin pressure and client concentration is material.
